# Privacy Policy – Eisenhauer Matrix

**Last updated: June 7, 2026**

## Overview

Eisenhauer Matrix ("we", "our", or "the app") is a Progressive Web App for task management using the Eisenhower Matrix method. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

---

## Data We Collect

### Guest Mode (no login)
- Tasks you create, stored **locally on your device only** (IndexedDB / localStorage)
- No personal data is collected or transmitted

### Signed-In Mode (Google Sign-In)
- **Google account data**: name, email address, profile picture (provided by Google OAuth)
- **Firebase User ID**: a unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication
- **Task data**: tasks you create are synced to Cloud Firestore (EU servers) under your User ID
- **Technical data**: app version, browser type (logged by Firebase for error handling)

---

## How We Use Your Data

| Data | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Google account info | Authentication, display your name | Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Task data | Core app functionality, cross-device sync | Contract performance |
| Firebase User ID | Identifying your data in Firestore | Contract performance |

---

## Data Storage & Third Parties

- **Firebase (Google LLC)**: Authentication + Firestore Database — servers in the EU
  - Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- **Google Sign-In**: OAuth authentication
  - Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **No advertising SDKs, no analytics, no data sold to third parties**

---

## Data Retention & Deletion

- Your task data is stored in Firestore as long as you use the app
- To delete all your data: use the "Account löschen" option in the app settings, or contact us via GitHub Issues
- Guest-mode data: stored locally on your device; deleted when you clear browser data or uninstall

---

## Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data (all task data is visible in the app)
- **Delete** your data (in-app account deletion or via contact)
- **Data portability** (export via in-app export function if available)
- **Lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority (e.g. Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz)

---

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Datenschutzerklärung – Eisenhauer Matrix

**Zuletzt aktualisiert: 7. Juni 2026**

## Übersicht

Eisenhauer Matrix ist eine Progressive Web App zur Aufgabenverwaltung nach der Eisenhower-Matrix-Methode. Diese Datenschutzerklärung erläutert den Umgang mit Ihren Daten.

---

## Erhobene Daten

### Gast-Modus (ohne Anmeldung)
- Von Ihnen erstellte Aufgaben, gespeichert **nur lokal auf Ihrem Gerät** (IndexedDB / localStorage)
- Keine personenbezogenen Daten werden erhoben oder übertragen

### Angemeldeter Modus (Google Sign-In)
- **Google-Konto-Daten**: Name, E-Mail-Adresse, Profilbild (bereitgestellt durch Google OAuth)
- **Firebase-Benutzer-ID**: eindeutige ID, generiert von Firebase Authentication
- **Aufgabendaten**: von Ihnen erstellte Aufgaben werden mit Cloud Firestore (EU-Server) unter Ihrer Benutzer-ID synchronisiert
- **Technische Daten**: App-Version, Browser-Typ (von Firebase für Fehlerbehandlung protokolliert)
